# Source paper

H. Duminil-Copin, V. Sidoravicius and V. Tassion,
*Continuity of the phase transition for planar random-cluster and Potts models with 1 ≤ q ≤ 4*,
arXiv:1505.04159v1 — <https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.04159v1>; published in
Comm. Math. Phys. **349** (2017), no. 1, 47–107, doi:10.1007/s00220-016-2759-8.

The paper is distributed by arXiv under its non-exclusive distribution licence and is therefore
**not** included in this repository. The formalization was checked against a 66-page PDF of
version 1 dated 15 May 2015, with SHA-256

    f32525e5b96e59e854850a8db77f579d2b5c82761e00d1ce45be454df62b4ad8

and against the TeX source of version 1 from the arXiv e-print. The PDF that arXiv serves for
version 1 is a separately generated build of the same version; on 2026-10-20 it had SHA-256

    7d2934e489afb571136b67ea887bea85835276ad3d80e9999343d05b7d9c1c99

`scripts/fetch-paper.sh` downloads the arXiv PDF of version 1 into this directory and compares its
hash with the second value (arXiv may occasionally regenerate PDFs; a mismatch is reported, not
hidden).
